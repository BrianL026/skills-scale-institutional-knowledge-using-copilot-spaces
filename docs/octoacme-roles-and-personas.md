# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and translate them into actionable requirements. They improve shared understanding of scope, constraints, and expected outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements, assumptions, and constraints
- Refine user stories and acceptance criteria with clear business context
- Identify process gaps, dependencies, and policy/compliance impacts
- Support traceability from business goals to delivered features

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Clarify requirements and edge cases during refinement and implementation.
- **Product Managers:** Align detailed requirements with product goals and prioritization.
- **Project Managers:** Surface scope dependencies and requirement risks that affect timeline and delivery planning.

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and deployment execution across teams. They ensure releases are predictable, communicated, and auditable.

### Responsibilities
- Define and run release readiness checklists and go/no-go criteria
- Coordinate release windows, cutover plans, and rollback readiness
- Track release dependencies, approvals, and environment readiness
- Communicate release status and post-release outcomes

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Confirm code freeze expectations, deployment requirements, and release blockers.
- **Product Managers:** Align release content with customer commitments and launch messaging.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate schedules, dependency sequencing, and stakeholder communications for release milestones.

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architecture and engineering direction to balance quality, speed, and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Own technical approach for key initiatives and high-risk changes
- Lead design reviews and enforce engineering standards
- Identify technical risks and define mitigation strategies
- Mentor developers and support estimation of technical complexity

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide design guidance, review implementation approaches, and unblock technical decisions.
- **Product Managers:** Evaluate feasibility and trade-offs between scope, quality, and delivery speed.
- **Project Managers:** Translate technical risks/dependencies into actionable delivery plans and milestones.

---

## Support/Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support/Operations Representatives contribute production and customer-impact insights to planning and execution. They help ensure operational readiness and fast issue response.

### Responsibilities
- Provide incident trends, support pain points, and operational constraints
- Validate monitoring, alerting, and runbook readiness for releases
- Participate in launch readiness and post-release monitoring
- Escalate production issues and customer-impact signals quickly

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Share production feedback, help reproduce issues, and verify fixes in operational workflows.
- **Product Managers:** Inform prioritization with customer-impact data and support volume trends.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate incident communication, readiness checkpoints, and response ownership during releases.

---

## UX Designers

### Role Summary
UX Designers define and validate user experience direction so delivered features are usable, consistent, and aligned to user goals.

### Responsibilities
- Create user flows, wireframes, and interaction designs
- Validate usability through research, prototypes, and feedback loops
- Define UX acceptance considerations and accessibility expectations
- Partner on design-system consistency and interaction quality

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Clarify interaction behavior and review implementation against design intent.
- **Product Managers:** Align user experience decisions with product outcomes and customer value.
- **Project Managers:** Plan design milestones, feedback cycles, and dependencies that affect delivery timing.
